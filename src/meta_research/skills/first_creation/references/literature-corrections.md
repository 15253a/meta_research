# Literature corrections

The accepted snapshot predates first Question confirmation. Read literature.json, papers.json, summary.md, and supplied full text when a claim needs it. Follow actual Reader evidence locators. Keep paper claims, cross-paper synthesis, and your inference distinct.

Each correction names a prior_statement_ref and a disposition of confirmed, qualified, contradicted, or unresolved. Explain the change. original_sources contains material keys from the original basis. literature_sources contains the actual paper_id and locator present in the accepted literature. The revised understanding retains the same material selection and source entrances. Adjust conditions and unfinished questions when evidence warrants it. Do not invent a correction for an honest empty result.

The first Question combines useful old work with new knowledge. It remains editable. The durable revision records the exact predecessor and snapshot, even if the human edits the Question or explicitly reviews a changed draft.
